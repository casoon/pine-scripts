# Wolfe Wave Scanner Pro

Searches a depth window of confirmed swings for every distinct, non-overlapping 5-point Wolfe Wave wedge — a contracting, alternating channel where point 5 tests the 1-3 trendline — not just the single highest-scoring one. Each pattern found projects its own EPA (Estimated Price at Arrival) target from line 1-4 at the time lines 1-3 and 2-4 converge, and is tracked live independently through its own lifecycle until it hits target, invalidates, or expires.

Wolfe Waves are strongly interpretation-dependent — small changes in swing strength or search window shift which geometry gets found. Treat this as a **structural setup finder with a quality score**, not a signal machine: "pattern found" means "a Wolfe-shaped geometry exists here, worth checking against context," not "enter now."

## Features

- Alternating swing engine (`ta.pivothigh`/`ta.pivotlow`) with bounded history
- Best-of-window search: every valid point-5 anchor within `Suchbereich letzte Swings` is tried (a Wolfe wave is often already several swings old by the time it completes), and for each, points 1-4 are chosen combinatorially from all valid non-consecutive swings — a wave spanning e.g. swings 1,3,5,7,9 is evaluated too, not just the literal last 5 swings
- Tracks up to `Max. gleichzeitige Patterns` distinct, non-overlapping valid patterns at once, each with its own drawings and invalidation state — a second genuinely separate Wolfe wave elsewhere on the chart is no longer discarded just because it scores lower than the first one found. An overlapping candidate only replaces an already-tracked pattern at that spot if it scores higher
- Continuous 0-100 score across nine criteria: structure, contraction, channel/traversal, apex convergence, directional point-5 zone, risk/reward, timing, leg-time symmetry, and leg purity — structure, contraction, channel, boundary hold, leg purity, P5-zone, and apex convergence are also hard-gated into validity, not just scored
- EPA target is the price on line 1-4 at the apex time (where lines 1-3 and 2-4 converge, both drawn bold and stopping exactly there — no dangling infinite extension). The 1-4 line itself is drawn visibly through points 1 and 4 up to that same apex bar, and the vertical apex marker drops from that exact point to point 5 — one shared point everywhere, label/dashboard/drawing included
- Live pattern lifecycle: each tracked pattern runs `ACTIVE` → `PARTIAL_SUCCESS` → `TARGET_HIT` / `EXPIRED` / `INVALIDATED`, with a live-updating EPA target line (recomputed from line 1-4 every bar), a one-time 50%-to-target partial marker, an `Ablaufpuffer nach ETA (Bars)` expiry cutoff, and a dynamic invalidation level that tracks the live 1-3 line for `Dynamische 1-3-Invalidierung (Bars)` bars before freezing — `Invalidierung nur per Close` (default on) chooses between a confirmed close beyond the level or an intrabar high/low breach
- Near-miss candidate marker (`Watch` signal, visual only, requires a real bull/bear structure) plus a dashed candidate wedge (same 1-3/2-4 boundaries as a real pattern) and a score-breakdown debug table for tuning thresholds
- Light-theme dashboard (status, active pattern count, best score, bias, live EPA, EPA @ ETA, invalidation level) — Bias/EPA/Invalidation reflect the best currently *tracked* pattern when one exists, the raw search candidate otherwise
- `Invalidierung anzeigen` (default off) draws the invalidation level line itself; invalidation *tracking* (the pattern still gets removed/alerted when the level breaks) always runs regardless of this toggle
- Optional `Reward als Pflichtfilter` (default off) to hard-require a minimum reward instead of only scoring it

## Scoring

| Criterion | Points | What it checks |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | 20 | Core, non-negotiable shape: sequence alternates high/low and point 3 undercuts/exceeds point 1, point 5 extends beyond point 3 (lows declining for a bull setup, highs rising for bear) |
| Contraction | 15 | Channel (line 2-4 vs. points 1/5) narrows by at least 30% end-to-start *and* narrows monotonically through the midpoint (no bulging outward around points 2-4); the 1-3 and 2-4 boundaries trend the same direction *and* their slope magnitudes are within a 3x ratio of each other (rules out a near-flat line paired with a near-vertical one). Quality maxes out once the contraction ratio reaches the 70% gate threshold and fades to 0 as the channel stops contracting at all |
| Channel | 10 | Point 4 sits at least 0.5 ATR off the 1-3 line, and the completed point-4-to-point-5 leg crosses at least 60% of the remaining channel width (`traversal45`) — a shape nicety, scored (and gated) separately so it can't zero out `Structure` on its own |
| Convergence (apex) | 10 | Lines 1-3 and 2-4 converge ahead of point 5, within `2×` the pattern's own width; quality peaks when the apex lands roughly 75% of the pattern's width past point 5 |
| P5-Zone | 15 | Point 5's directional offset from the 1-3 trendline: an overshoot beyond the line, up to `P5 max. Overshoot ATR` (default 1.0), scores highest around a modest 0.5 ATR overshoot; staying inside the wedge, up to `P5 max. innerhalb ATR` (default 0.75), scores lower the deeper inside it sits |
| Risk/Reward | 15 | Reward (EPA target vs. point 5, capped to `Max. Zielentfernung ATR-Multiple`) divided by risk (point 5 vs. the invalidation level); quality scales linearly with the resulting R-multiple, capped at 2R |
| Timing | 5 | Legs are monotonically spaced, the final leg isn't disproportionately long relative to the rest, and no single leg (including a middle one) consumes more than 40% of the pattern's total duration; quality maxes out once the largest leg is at or under 25% of total duration and fades to 0 as it approaches 50% |
| Symmetry | 5 | Corresponding legs (1-2 vs. 3-4, 2-3 vs. 4-5) don't diverge too far in duration; quality fades to 0 as the divergence ratio approaches the 45% gate threshold |
| Leg Purity | 5 | No swing skipped by the combinatorial point selection deviates from its enclosing leg's straight path by more than `Max. Zwischen-Swing / Leg` (default 50%) of that leg's own amplitude — rejects a "clean" 5-point chain that quietly ignores a near-equal countermove |

Every criterion above is continuous (0-100% of its own points), not a binary pass/fail split. A pattern only fires when, in addition to the weighted score clearing `Mindestqualität`, every one of the following hard gates also passes: `Structure`, `Contraction`, `Channel`, `Boundary Hold` (below), `Bar Boundary` (below), `Leg Purity`, `P5-Zone`, and `Convergence` — so the seq-agnostic criteria (`Timing`, `Symmetry`) can no longer carry a broken channel or a non-converging apex across the line on score alone. `Reward als Pflichtfilter` (off by default) additionally requires `Risk/Reward` to clear `Mindest-Risk/Reward` (default 1.5) instead of just contributing its 15 points.

**Boundary Hold (veto, not scored).** A real Wolfe wave only tests the 1-3 trendline once, at point 5 — but the channel has to hold on *both* sides, not just that one. The scanner checks every swing strictly between points 1 and 5 — including ones the combinatorial search skipped when picking a non-consecutive point selection — against both the 1-3 *and* the 2-4 boundary, using `Swing-Boundary-Toleranz ATR` (default 0.35). If price already broke through either boundary earlier (e.g. a sharp plunge-and-recovery between points 3 and 4 on the 1-3 side, or a breach of the 2-4 side that never gets its own dedicated "test" the way point 5 tests 1-3), the pattern is rejected outright: the five chosen points can score well on their own while the actual price path between them already violated the channel the wave depends on.

**Bar Boundary (veto, not scored).** A separate, finer check (`Bar-Boundary-Toleranz ATR`, default 0.25) scans every single *bar* — not just the registered swings — between points 1 and 5 against both boundaries, catching a poke through the channel that never formed its own swing. Point 5's own bar is exempt from the 1-3 side of this check (it's graded by `P5-Zone` instead, which allows a deliberate overshoot); the 2-4 wall still applies to it like any other bar.

**P5-Zone is directional and gated, not just scored.** Point 5 is no longer measured as a symmetric distance from the 1-3 line: overshooting beyond it and staying inside the wedge are graded — and tolerated — differently, via separate `P5 max. Overshoot ATR` and `P5 max. innerhalb ATR` inputs. A point 5 outside either tolerance isn't testing the trendline, it's blowing through it (or never reaching it) — the same class of violation as a mid-pattern boundary break, just at the last point instead of an earlier one.

**Channel is gated, not just scored.** `Channel` also hard-blocks `valid` now: point 4 must be a meaningful opposite-boundary excursion (≥0.5 ATR off the 1-3 line) and the point-4-to-point-5 leg must cross most (≥60%) of the remaining channel — a point 5 that ends up deep inside the channel instead of completing the traversal to the opposite wall isn't a finished wedge.

**Leg Purity is gated, not just scored.** The combinatorial search can select a "clean" chain of 5 points while skipping an intermediate swing that moved almost as far as the leg it's nested inside — effectively hiding a real countermove. `Leg Purity` rejects any selection where a skipped swing's deviation from its enclosing leg's straight path exceeds `Max. Zwischen-Swing / Leg` (default 50%) of that leg's own amplitude.

**Convergence (apex) is gated, not just scored.** `Convergence` also hard-blocks `valid` now: if the 1-3/2-4 wedge has already converged at or before point 5 (`etaOk` false), the pattern isn't a completed Wolfe wave — and drawing it anyway is actively misleading, since the fallback apex projection extrapolates the same already-converged slopes further out and can go wildly, even negatively, off in price.

## Pattern Lifecycle

Once a pattern fires it enters state `ACTIVE` and is tracked, every bar, independently of every other tracked pattern. Each bar it's checked against three mutually exclusive terminal states, in this order, plus one interim marker:

- **INVALIDATED** — the invalidation level breaks. For the first `Dynamische 1-3-Invalidierung (Bars)` bars (default 10) after point 5, that level tracks `min/max(point 5, the live 1-3 line) ± Invalidierungs-Puffer ATR` (default 0.5 ATR) bar by bar; afterwards it freezes at its last value. Depending on `Invalidierung nur per Close` (default on), either a confirmed close beyond the level or an intrabar high/low breach triggers this. Fires `WWSP · INVALIDATED`.
- **TARGET_HIT** — price reaches the *live* EPA target, recomputed from line 1-4 every bar (not the frozen apex projection used for scoring/drawing). Fires `WWSP · EPA TARGET HIT`.
- **EXPIRED** — price hasn't hit the target within `Ablaufpuffer nach ETA (Bars)` (default 10) bars past the pattern's computed apex bar. Fires `WWSP · EXPIRED`.
- **PARTIAL_SUCCESS** — a one-time interim marker that does *not* remove the pattern from tracking: fires once price reaches the halfway point between point 5 and the live EPA target, on any bar that didn't already exit into one of the three states above. Fires `WWSP · PARTIAL SUCCESS`.

Any of the three terminal states draws a label, removes the pattern from tracking (freeing a slot for a new one), and fires its alert; `Abgeschlossene/ersetzte Patterns behalten` controls whether that pattern's drawings are deleted at that point or kept on the chart as history.

## Multiple patterns

`Max. gleichzeitige Patterns` (default 5, capped at 8) caps how many valid patterns are tracked and drawn at once — each owns up to ~9 lines and 6 labels, so the cap also bounds drawing-object growth (`Suchbereich letzte Swings` is similarly capped at 24 to bound the combinatorial search cost). Each search pass finds the best-scoring valid combination *per point-5 anchor* in the window, not just the single overall best — so two non-overlapping Wolfe waves elsewhere on the chart both surface instead of only the higher-scoring one. Overlap is checked on the [point 4, point 5] completion leg only, not the full [point 1, point 5] span — since point 1 can sit anywhere within `Suchbereich letzte Swings`, two well-separated completions often still share the same early swings for points 1-2, and comparing the full range would let one wide pattern block every other candidate in that stretch. A margin — a third of the larger pattern's own total width — is added to both patterns' ranges before comparing, so two same-direction patterns crammed a few bars apart still count as overlapping even if their exact completion legs don't literally touch. Overlap only ever competes patterns of the *same direction* for a slot — a bull and a bear pattern occupying the same bars are two different scenarios, not rivals. If a new candidate's completion leg overlaps an already-tracked, same-direction pattern's, it only replaces it when it scores higher (and isn't the exact same pattern already tracked); otherwise it's skipped. After every search, a cleanup pass checks all tracked patterns pairwise and removes the lower-scoring one of any same-direction, overlapping pair still left over — this catches overlap chains (A overlaps B, B overlaps C, but A and C don't directly overlap) that the one-at-a-time insertion check alone can miss.

Once all `Max. gleichzeitige Patterns` slots are full, a new non-overlapping candidate evicts the currently weakest (lowest-scoring) tracked pattern if it scores higher — otherwise the first patterns to fire that happen not to invalidate for a while would permanently occupy every slot and silently block every better candidate found afterward. `Abgeschlossene/ersetzte Patterns behalten` controls whether a pattern's drawings are deleted once it's invalidated, hits target, expires, is replaced, or is evicted, or kept on the chart as history.

## Debug

Enabling `Score-Breakdown Debug-Tabelle` shows a table with the current best-in-depth candidate's pass/fail state per criterion and its running score against `Mindestqualität` — useful for tuning thresholds without guessing why a candidate didn't fire. `Kandidat-Mindestscore (Watch)` controls the near-miss marker's lower bound.

Enabling `Pine-Logs aktivieren (Fehlersuche)` (off by default) emits `WWSP SWING`/`WWSP SEARCH`/`WWSP PATTERN FIRED`/`WWSP PATTERN REPLACED`/`WWSP PATTERN EVICTED`/`WWSP INVALIDATED`/`WWSP TARGET HIT`/`WWSP EXPIRED`/`WWSP PARTIAL` lines to the Pine Logs pane, including how many 5-point combinations were evaluated per search and the full per-criterion breakdown (including `boundaryHold`) of the single best-in-window candidate — useful when a symbol/timeframe combination seems to produce no signals at all, or to check why a specific candidate wasn't tracked (score too low, boundary broken prematurely, replaced by/skipped in favor of an overlapping higher-scoring one, or skipped because the cap was full and it didn't beat the weakest tracked pattern).
